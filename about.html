About peptide.diy

Our mission, our standards, and why we built this resource.

Our Mission

peptide.diy exists because the information gap in the peptide research space is enormous. Researchers — whether working in academic labs, conducting self-experimentation, or simply trying to make informed decisions — face a landscape where most available information comes from unverified forums, vendor marketing, or clinical blogs that don't cite their sources.

We built this site to provide what the field was missing: protocols grounded in published science, with clear citations, honest safety information, and the practical details that make the difference between a well-executed protocol and a guessing game.

Our Standards

  • Every dosage and mechanism claim is sourced from peer-reviewed literature, FDA labeling, or established clinical references
  • Citations are linked to primary sources — we don't cite other websites or blogs as evidence
  • Safety information is included on every protocol page, not buried or omitted
  • We are not affiliated with any peptide vendor, clinic, or supplement company
  • Content is reviewed and updated when new research warrants it

What We Are and Are Not

We are: An educational and research reference site. Our goal is to make scientific information about peptides accurate, accessible, and practically useful.

We are not: A medical provider. Nothing on this site is medical advice or a substitute for consultation with a licensed healthcare professional. We do not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

We are not: A vendor. We do not sell peptides, supplies, or any physical products. Where we reference vendors, we do so as third-party information only.

How to Use This Site

If you're new to peptide research, start with our Beginner's Guide to understand the fundamentals. When you're ready to look at specific compounds, browse our Single Peptide Protocols — each page includes everything you need in one place.

If something isn't clear, or if you need a protocol for a compound we don't yet cover, contact us. We add new content regularly based on user requests.
