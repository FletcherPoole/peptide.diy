About peptide.diy

Our mission, standards, and why we built this resource.

Our Mission

peptide.diy exists because good peptide information is hard to find. Whether you're in a lab, running your own experiments, or just trying to sort fact from hype, most of what's online comes from forums, vendors, or "clinical" blogs that never cite a source.

We built this site to bring clarity to the field: protocols grounded in published science, with clear citations, honest safety information, and the practical details that make the difference between a well-executed protocol and a guessing game.

Our Standards

  • Every dosage and mechanism claim is sourced from peer-reviewed literature, FDA prescribing information, or established clinical references
  • Citations link to primary sources. We don't treat other websites or blogs as evidence.
  • Safety information is included on every protocol page, not buried or omitted
  • Content is reviewed and updated when new research warrants it

What We Are and Are Not

We are: An educational and research reference site. Our goal is to make scientific information about peptides accurate, accessible, and practically useful.

We are not: A medical provider. Nothing on this site is medical advice or a substitute for consultation with a licensed healthcare professional. We do not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

We are not: A vendor. We do not sell peptides, supplies, or any physical products, and no money is paid to peptide.diy for access or referrals. Where we reference vendors, we do so to provide trusted and safe sources to protect readers.

How to Use This Site

If you're new to peptide research, start with our Beginner's Guide to understand the fundamentals. When you're ready to look at specific compounds, browse our Single Peptide Protocols. Each page has dosing, reconstitution, cycle notes, and references in one place.

If something isn't clear, or if you need a protocol for a compound we don't yet cover, contact us. We add new content regularly based on user requests.
