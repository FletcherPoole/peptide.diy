CJC-1295 (No DAC) + Ipamorelin 10mg Blend

Growth-hormone secretagogue blend: reconstitution math and dosing schedules.

Protocol Overview

This 10mg blend contains CJC-1295 No DAC (5mg) + Ipamorelin (5mg) in a single vial. CJC-1295 No DAC (also called Modified GRF(1-29)) is a GHRH analogue that amplifies the pituitary's natural GH pulses. Ipamorelin is a selective GHRP (ghrelin receptor agonist) that triggers GH release with minimal impact on cortisol or prolactin.

These two peptides work together: CJC-1295 No DAC amplifies the magnitude of GH pulses while Ipamorelin stimulates the pituitary through a different receptor pathway. Their combination can produce significantly higher GH release than either peptide alone. The short half-life of CJC-1295 No DAC (~30 min) means the blend is typically injected 2–3 times daily.

Reconstitution

Recommended BAC water: 2 mL. Resulting concentration: 10 mg ÷ 2 mL = 5 mg/mL total (2.5 mg/mL each peptide).
