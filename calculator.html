How the math works

  1. 1

    Concentration

    Divide total peptide by BAC water volume → mg/mL (or IU/mL).

  2. 2

    Draw volume

    Divide your dose by concentration → mL to pull per injection.

  3. 3

    Syringe units

    Multiply mL by your syringe scale (U-100: 1 mL = 100 units).
