## protocol-callout (Research purposes only)
- **Research purposes only.** Tirzepatide is a **prescription drug** (Mounjaro/Zepbound, Eli Lilly), a **GLP-1/GIP dual receptor agonist** — NOT approved for DIY/self-directed use.
- Carries an **FDA boxed warning** (thyroid C-cell tumours). Educational information; **not medical advice.** Titration and use belong under medical supervision.

---

## At a Glance (protocol-tldr)
- **Vial:** 30 mg lyophilised tirzepatide (research vial). Siblings: 10 mg, 15 mg, 60 mg.
- **Mix:** 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water → **10.0 mg/mL** (30 ÷ 3.0 = 10.0 mg/mL).
- **Dose:** weekly SC; start **2.5 mg**, titrate by 2.5 mg every 4 weeks as tolerated; typical maintenance **5–15 mg/week**.
- **Cycle:** continuous weekly dosing; effect is maintained only while dosing continues (weight regain common on cessation — see Research).
- **Route:** subcutaneous (abdomen/thigh/upper arm, rotate sites), once weekly, with or without food.
- **Per dose (at 10 mg/mL):** 2.5 mg = 0.25 mL = **25 U**; 5 mg = 0.50 mL = **50 U**; 7.5 mg = 0.75 mL = **75 U**; 10 mg = 1.0 mL = **100 U**.
- **Consider:** slow titration to limit nausea/vomiting; antiemetic-friendly meals; hydration; contraindicated in personal/family MTC or MEN2.
- **Sibling vial link:** smaller/larger recon in the 10 mg / 60 mg pages.

## Cycle / Titration
- Intro: schedule below **mirrors the FDA Mounjaro label titration** (2.5 mg start, +2.5 mg every ≥4 weeks; max 15 mg/week) — cited, not anecdotal. Research vials are unregulated; numbers assume 10 mg/mL.
- **Standard titration table** (30 mg vial @ 10 mg/mL):

| Phase | Duration | Weekly dose | Volume | U-100 units | Doses left in 30 mg vial* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initiation | Weeks 1–4 | 2.5 mg | 0.25 mL | 25 U | 12 at this dose |
| Escalation 1 | Weeks 5–8 | 5 mg | 0.50 mL | 50 U | 6 at this dose |
| Escalation 2 | Weeks 9–12 | 7.5 mg | 0.75 mL | 4 at this dose | 75 U |
| Escalation 3 / Maint. | Weeks 13–16 | 10 mg | 1.0 mL | 100 U | 3 at this dose |
| Optional | Weeks 17+ | 12.5 mg | 1.25 mL | 125 U (>1 syringe) | — |
| Max | Weeks 21+ | 15 mg | 1.5 mL | 150 U (>1 syringe) | — |

- *Doses-per-vial is per single dose level — a real titration mixes levels, so one 30 mg vial spans several early weeks. Fix U-100 column typo in table (7.5 mg row = 75 U).
- **Vial longevity:** 30 mg vial = 3.0 mL total. Reconstituted stability ~**28 days refrigerated (2–8 °C)** — plan the vial around the 4-week window, not just the maths.
- **Do NOT jump doses** to chase results — GI adverse events are dose- and escalation-driven (see Research).

- **Tirzepatide vials** — 30 mg × qty for planned weeks (or mix 10/15/30/60 mg to match target dose).
- **Bacteriostatic water** — 3 mL per 30 mg vial (multi-use, benzyl-alcohol preserved) + spare.

- **General supplies `<ul>`:** sharps bin, gauze, sterile storage rack, fridge thermometer, label/date sticker for reconstitution date.

## Dosing & Reconstitution

### Reconstitution
- **Recommended:** 30 mg vial + **3.0 mL** bacteriostatic water → **10.0 mg/mL** (compounding-standard concentration).
- Numbered mixing steps:
  1. Swab both stoppers; let dry.
  2. Draw 3.0 mL BAC water.
  3. Inject slowly down the **inside wall** of the vial — do not blast onto the powder.
  4. Swirl gently; **do not shake** (peptide is shear-sensitive). Leave until fully clear (a few minutes).
  5. Label with date; refrigerate.
- **Stability:** store reconstituted **2–8 °C, use within ~28 days**; do not freeze after reconstitution. Lyophilised vial: freezer (−20 °C), protect from light/moisture.
- Link → Storage Guide.

### Injectable dosing table (10 mg/mL)

| Level | Weekly dose | Volume | U-100 units | Doses per 30 mg vial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 2.5 mg | 0.25 mL | 25 U | 12 |
| Low | 5 mg | 0.50 mL | 50 U | 6 |
| Mid | 7.5 mg | 0.75 mL | 75 U | 4 |
| Maint. | 10 mg | 1.0 mL | 100 U | 3 |
| High | 12.5 mg | 1.25 mL | 125 U | ~2 |
| Max | 15 mg | 1.5 mL | 150 U | 2 |

- **Formula:** `Volume (mL) = dose (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/mL)` → `U-100 units = Volume (mL) × 100`.
- **Worked example (5 mg at 10 mg/mL):** 5 ÷ 10 = 0.50 mL → 0.50 × 100 = **50 units** on a U-100 syringe.
- **Alt concentration note:** a 10 mg vial in 1.0 mL also = 10 mg/mL; a 10 mg vial in 2.0 mL = 5 mg/mL (then units double — e.g. 2.5 mg = 50 U). Keep concentration consistent to avoid dosing errors.
- **calc callout:** "Open this 30 mg vial in the calculator" → prefilled method injection / 30 mg / 3 mL BAC.

## Alternative Protocols
- **Smaller vials (10 / 15 mg):** for lower total exposure or trialling; 10 mg @ 1.0 mL = 10 mg/mL keeps the same unit maths. `<h3>` per size.
- **Larger vial (60 mg):** reconstitute 6.0 mL → 10 mg/mL; fewer vials for long maintenance; more product at risk if contaminated.
- **Slower "micro-titration" [ANECDOTAL]:** community reports of 2.5 mg → holding longer than 4 weeks, or 1.25 mg half-steps, to reduce nausea. Not label-supported; label minimum step is 2.5 mg.
- **Class analogs (context, not interchange):** semaglutide (GLP-1 only), retatrutide (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple agonist). Different potency/titration — do not swap unit-for-unit.

## What Is Tirzepatide
- Synthetic **39-amino-acid peptide**, once-weekly; **dual agonist of GIP and GLP-1 receptors** (developed as LY3298176, Eli Lilly).
- Mechanism: enhances **glucose-dependent insulin secretion**, suppresses glucagon, **slows gastric emptying**, and **reduces appetite/food intake**.
- **Half-life ~5 days** → weekly dosing. Marketed as **Mounjaro** (type 2 diabetes) and **Zepbound** (chronic weight management).
- Data maturity: **strong human RCT evidence** (SURPASS diabetes programme; SURMOUNT obesity programme) — unusually mature vs most research peptides.

---

## Supplementary Notes
- **Combinations [ANECDOTAL]:** stacked with training/calorie deficit for body-comp; some pair with metformin. Combining with insulin/sulfonylureas raises **hypoglycaemia risk** — dose reductions of the other agent may be needed (label caution).
- **Interactions:** delayed gastric emptying can alter absorption of oral drugs; **oral contraceptive** effectiveness may drop around initiation/escalation (label advises backup method).
- **Muscle loss:** rapid weight loss includes lean mass — resistance training + adequate protein commonly advised **[ANECDOTAL]**.
- **Side effects (SC, dose/titration-driven):**
  - Very common GI: **nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting, constipation, dyspepsia, decreased appetite** — mostly mild–moderate, worst during escalation.
  - Injection-site reactions; fatigue.
  - Serious/rare: **pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, acute kidney injury** (via dehydration from vomiting), **severe hypoglycaemia** (with insulin/secretagogues), hypersensitivity, possible diabetic retinopathy change.
- **Contraindications:** personal/family history of **medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC)** or **MEN2**; prior serious hypersensitivity. Caution: history of **pancreatitis**, severe GI disease, pregnancy/breastfeeding.

## Research (cited, linked)
- **Phase 2 diabetes (dose-finding):** Frias et al., *Lancet* 2018 — 26-wk, 1/5/10/15 mg vs dulaglutide/placebo; HbA1c −1.06% to −1.94%; weight −0.9 to −11.3 kg; GI AEs dose-related (nausea/diarrhoea/vomiting up to ~66% at 15 mg); no severe hypoglycaemia. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30293770/
- **SURPASS-2 (vs semaglutide, T2D):** 40-wk, 5/10/15 mg vs semaglutide 1 mg; HbA1c −2.01/−2.24/−2.30%; weight −7.6/−9.3/−11.2 kg; superior to semaglutide. https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-surpass-2-results-published-new-england-journal-medicine
- **SURMOUNT-1 (obesity, non-diabetic):** Jastreboff et al., *NEJM* 2022 — 72-wk; weight loss **16.0% (5 mg), 21.4% (10 mg), 22.5% (15 mg)** vs 2.4% placebo; ≥5% loss in 89–96%; nausea 24.6–33.3%, vomiting 8.3–12.2%, diarrhoea 18.7–23.0%; AE discontinuation 4.3–7.1% (2.6% placebo). https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038
- **Mechanism/review:** Gallwitz, *Frontiers in Endocrinology* 2022. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2022.1004044
- **Reference monograph:** Farzam & Patel, *Tirzepatide*, StatPearls. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK585056/
- **Regulatory dosing/safety of record:** FDA Mounjaro Prescribing Information (boxed warning, titration, contraindications). https://pi.lilly.com/us/mounjaro-uspi.pdf
- **callout — Research vs anecdote:** trial doses (2.5–15 mg) and titration are label/RCT-backed; "faster is better", half-step micro-titration, and stacking claims are community anecdote.

## Beyond weight loss
- **Type 2 diabetes / glycaemic control**
  - **Research:** SURPASS programme — large, dose-dependent HbA1c reductions, superiority vs semaglutide 1 mg (SURPASS-2). https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-surpass-2-results-published-new-england-journal-medicine
  - **[ANECDOTAL]:** users report fasting-glucose improvements within weeks of titration.
- **Cardiometabolic / blood pressure & lipids**
  - **Research:** SURPASS analyses report systolic BP reductions alongside weight loss. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10039543/
  - **[ANECDOTAL]:** improved energy/appetite control reported; not a substitute for cardiology care.
- **Weight-loss durability / regain**
  - **Research:** long-term SURMOUNT follow-up shows benefit sustained **while dosing continues**; stopping is associated with partial regain. https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038
  - **[ANECDOTAL]:** community consensus that maintenance dosing (or careful taper) is needed to hold results.
