## 1. Protocol callout (Research purposes only)
- Melanocortin receptor agonist. Bremelanotide IS FDA-approved (Vyleesi, 2019) for premenopausal HSDD at **1.75mg subQ on-demand** — that label anchors the clinical reference dose (see Research). Our **[HOUSE PROTOCOL]** below runs lower daily/route-based bands.
- Transient blood pressure rise: **caution / avoid in uncontrolled hypertension or known cardiovascular disease** (label contraindication).
- Educational, not medical advice.

## 2. At a Glance (protocol-tldr)
- **Vial:** 10mg lyophilised PT-141 (bremelanotide acetate).
- **Mix:** 2mL bacteriostatic water → **5mg/mL (5000mcg/mL)**. At this strength **1 U-100 unit = 50mcg**.
- **Three routes, three dose bands** (house):
  - **Inject (subQ):** 125–1000mcg/day → **2.5–20 units** at 5mg/mL.
  - **Troche (sublingual):** 20–40+ IU, on-demand or daily.
  - **Nasal spray:** 1–4+ sprays/day (~250mcg per spray).
- **Clinical anchor:** the FDA Vyleesi dose is **1.75mg subQ on-demand** — reference only, not the house daily band.
- **Consider:** face flushing and nausea are the common short-lived effects; melanocortin skin-darkening possible with frequent dosing.
- Sibling / related: PT-141 nasal spray; Melanotan II (related melanocortin analogue).

## 3. Cycle / usage pattern
- **House pattern = flexible: daily is fine on all three routes,** or on-demand before activity (troche especially suits on-demand at 20 IU).
- **Tolerance:** you may build a small tolerance over a long run of daily use — take **3–7 days off** and you resensitise rapidly (see FAQ).
- **Titrate up, not straight to the top:** start at each route's Low band to gauge flushing/nausea and BP response, then move to Average/High if well tolerated.
- Vial longevity depends on route and dose — at 250mcg/day subQ a 10mg vial ≈ 40 days; reconstituted solution keeps ~30 days refrigerated, so match recon volume to how fast you'll use it.

## 4. Supplies (supply-grid: 4 cards + footnote)
- **Vial(s):** 1 × PT-141 10mg (covers weeks of daily subQ, or many on-demand uses).
- **Bacteriostatic water:** 1 × 30mL BAC (2mL per vial recon; plenty of headroom).
- **Syringes:** U-100 insulin syringes (0.5mL/29–31G) — house subQ draws are small (**2.5–20 units**).
- **Alcohol swabs:** for vial stopper + injection site.
- Footnote: troche and nasal-spray routes need no syringe once dosed; rotate subQ sites; store reconstituted vial refrigerated.
- General supplies `<ul>`: sharps bin, gauze, optional troche moulds / nasal atomiser depending on route.

## 5. Dosing & Reconstitution

### Reconstitution (all routes share the same recon)
- Recommended: **10mg vial + 2mL BAC water = 5mg/mL (5000mcg/mL)**.
- At 5mg/mL, **1 U-100 unit (0.01mL) = 50mcg**. Keep this in mind for every injection figure below.
- Add BAC slowly down the vial wall; swirl gently, do NOT shake; let fully dissolve.
- Refrigerate (2–8°C) after mixing; use within ~30 days; avoid freeze–thaw. Store lyophilised vial cold/dark until use.

### Route A — Injection (subQ) [HOUSE PROTOCOL]
- Owner's daily mcg bands converted to per-injection volume + U-100 units at **5mg/mL**:

| Level | Dose (per day) | Volume | U-100 units |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 125mcg | 0.025mL | 2.5 u |
| Average | 250mcg | 0.05mL | 5 u |
| High | 500mcg | 0.10mL | 10 u |
| High (upper) | 1000mcg | 0.20mL | 20 u |

- Inject subcutaneously (abdomen/thigh); rotate sites. High band = 500–1000mcg/day = **10–20 units**.

### Route B — Troche (sublingual) [HOUSE PROTOCOL]

| Level | Dose |
|---|---|
| Low | 20 IU on-demand (before sexual activity) |
| Average | 20 IU/day |
| High | 40 IU/day or more |

- **Place the troche under the tongue and let it dissolve (sublingual).**
- If it causes stomach distress, take with food and/or water.
- If nausea is intolerable, consider **Ondansetron** as a quick fix.

### Route C — Nasal spray [HOUSE PROTOCOL]

| Level | Dose | Approx PT-141 |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 1 spray/day | ~250mcg/day |
| Average | 2–3 sprays/day | ~500–750mcg/day |
| High | 4+ sprays/day | ~1000mcg+/day |

- ~250mcg delivered per spray (nasal absorption is lower and more variable than subQ — see Alternatives/Research).

### Formulae (injection route)
- **Volume (mL) = dose (mcg) ÷ 5000** (concentration in mcg/mL)
- **U-100 units = Volume (mL) × 100** — or simply **units = dose (mcg) ÷ 50**
- Worked example: 250mcg ÷ 50 = **5 units** = 0.05mL. 1000mcg ÷ 50 = **20 units** = 0.20mL.

### Timing & kinetics (cited — see Research)
- On-demand uses (troche Low, or before activity): dose **~45 min before** anticipated activity.
- Tmax ~1h; half-life ~2.7h; subQ bioavailability ~100%. Daily house dosing keeps steadier exposure than single on-demand hits.

## 6. Alternative / cross-reference notes (h3 per variant)
- **Nasal spray** — cross-link to PT-141 nasal spray page. Bioavailability is **lower and more variable** than subQ (historical intranasal trials, Diamond et al.); house nasal band runs higher per-use to compensate. Less predictable than injection.
- **Male use (off-label)** — [RESEARCH-adjacent] early intranasal male-ED trials and PT-141 + sildenafil co-dosing showed enhanced erectile response; Vyleesi approval is women-only, male use is off-label/community.
- **Melanotan II** — related MC agonist (tanning + libido); different risk profile, cross-link only, do not conflate.

## 7. What Is PT-141
- Synthetic cyclic heptapeptide; analogue of α-MSH; metabolite of Melanotan II.
- **Melanocortin receptor agonist** — non-selective, potency order MC1R > MC4R > MC3R > MC5R > MC2R; MC3R/MC4R are CNS receptors driving sexual response.
- Acts **centrally** (hypothalamic/limbic) on desire/arousal pathways — mechanistically distinct from PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra) which act on peripheral vasculature.
- Developed by Palatin; approved as **Vyleesi (2019)** for acquired, generalised HSDD in premenopausal women.
- Data maturity: strong human RCT data in premenopausal women (RECONNECT); male/other uses are earlier-stage or off-label.

## 8. Safety & Side effects
- **Common, short-lived effects (house note):** temporary **face flushing (red face)**, **nausea**, and **appetite suppression** are the most common. For most people this lasts only minutes to a couple of hours. If intolerable, lower your dose or stop and they resolve. For intolerable nausea, consider **Ondansetron** as a quick fix.
- **Side effects (clinical, route-split):**
  - subQ (from RECONNECT Phase 3): **nausea ~40%** (vs 1% placebo), **flushing ~20%**, injection-site reactions ~13%, **headache ~11%**.
  - **Transient BP rise (cardiovascular caution):** ~6mmHg systolic / 3mmHg diastolic, peaks 2–4h, resolves by ~12h. **Avoid in uncontrolled hypertension or known cardiovascular disease.**
  - **Focal hyperpigmentation (skin darkening):** ~1% at ≤8 doses/month, but **~38% with frequent/daily dosing** — the main trade-off of running a daily house band. May not fully resolve.
  - Nasal: BP effects historically more variable.
- **Contraindications:** uncontrolled hypertension; known cardiovascular disease.

## 9. FAQ (house voice)
- **Can I use this every day?**
  - Yes. You may develop a small tolerance over a long period, but after **3–7 days off** it you will resensitise rapidly.
- **What are the side effects?**
  - Temporary **face flushing (red face)**, **nausea**, and **appetite suppression** are the most common. For most people this lasts only minutes to a couple of hours. If intolerable, lower your dose or stop and they will go away. If nausea is intolerable, consider **Ondansetron** as a quick fix.
- **Which route should I pick?**
  - Injection (subQ) is the most predictable and lets you dial exact units; the sublingual troche suits on-demand use before activity; the nasal spray is the most convenient but the least predictable on absorption.
- **How does the injection dose map to units?**
  - At 5mg/mL, **1 unit = 50mcg**. So 250mcg = 5 units, 500mcg = 10 units, 1000mcg = 20 units.

## 10. Research (clinical anchor vs house protocol)
- **Clinical/label anchor:** the FDA-approved on-demand dose is **1.75mg subQ, ~45 min before activity, max 1/24h and ≤8/month** (Vyleesi label). This is the studied, approved reference point — **the house daily/route bands above are [HOUSE PROTOCOL], not the label dose.**
- **HSDD efficacy (women):** RECONNECT Phase 3 (Studies 301/302), n=1,247, 1.75mg subQ on-demand 24 wks → statistically significant rise in FSFI-desire and drop in FSDS-DAO distress vs placebo (P<0.001). Kingsberg et al., 2019.
- **Mechanism / pharmacology:** Molinoff et al., 2003 — PT-141 melanocortin agonism, dose-dependent erectile activity in men + preclinical.
- **Female arousal (intranasal):** Diamond et al., 2006 — single intranasal dose increased subjective arousal in premenopausal women with arousal disorder.
- **Regulatory anchor:** FDA Vyleesi label — 1.75mg subQ dosing, kinetics, safety, BP and hyperpigmentation warnings.
- Close with callout: "Research vs house protocol — approved data is for 1.75mg on-demand subQ in women; the house route bands (daily subQ, troche, nasal) are community practice, not label-validated."

## 11. Beyond libido (melanocortin effects — Research/Anecdotal split)
- **Appetite / body weight:**
  - Research: MC4R agonism modulates energy balance; melanocortin pathway is central to appetite regulation.
  - Anecdotal / house: mild **appetite suppression** is a common short-term effect (see Safety/FAQ); not a weight-loss tool, not validated.
- **Skin pigmentation:**
  - Research: MC1R activation drives melanogenesis → the observed focal hyperpigmentation (label).
  - Anecdotal: mild tanning/freckle-darkening reported, dose/frequency-dependent.
- **Mood / motivation:**
  - Research: central melanocortin tone linked to arousal/motivation circuits (preclinical).
  - Anecdotal: some report transient mood/energy lift; unproven.
