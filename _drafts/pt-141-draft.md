## 1. Protocol callout (Research purposes only)
- Melanocortin receptor agonist. Bremelanotide IS FDA-approved (Vyleesi, 2019) for premenopausal HSDD at **1.75mg subQ** — that approval anchors a safe reference dose, but generic/research PT-141 is unapproved and unregulated.
- Transient blood pressure rise: **caution / avoid in uncontrolled hypertension or known cardiovascular disease** (label contraindication).
- Not medical advice.

## 2. At a Glance (protocol-tldr)
- **Vial:** 10mg lyophilised PT-141 (bremelanotide acetate).
- **Mix:** 2mL bacteriostatic water → **5mg/mL (5000mcg/mL)**. (Alt recon options in Dosing section.)
- **Dose (reference):** 1.75mg subQ — the FDA-approved Vyleesi dose. Research/community often lower: **0.5–2mg**.
- **Timing:** inject **~45 min before** anticipated activity.
- **Frequency:** on-demand; **max 1 dose / 24h**, **≤8 doses / month** (per label).
- **Route:** subcutaneous (abdomen/thigh) preferred; nasal spray is an alternative (lower, more variable absorption — see Alternatives).
- **Consider:** nausea is common; melanocortin skin-darkening possible with frequent use.
- Sibling / related: PT-141 nasal spray; Melanotan II (related melanocortin analogue).

## 3. Cycle / usage pattern
- **Primary pattern = on-demand (episodic), NOT daily.** This is how it is approved and studied. Take one dose ~45 min before activity, no more than once per 24h, up to 8 times a month.
- Anecdotal "titration" / desensitisation-avoidance: start low (0.5–1mg) first use to gauge nausea and BP response, then adjust up toward 1.75–2mg if tolerated.
- Standard on-demand table (at 5mg/mL):

| Use pattern | Dose per injection | U-100 units | Volume | Approx doses per 10mg vial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low / first-time | 0.5mg | 10 u | 0.10mL | ~20 |
| Common | 1mg | 20 u | 0.20mL | ~10 |
| Reference (FDA) | 1.75mg | 35 u | 0.35mL | ~5–6 |
| Upper | 2mg | 40 u | 0.40mL | ~5 |

- Vial longevity: a 10mg vial = roughly 5–6 doses at the 1.75mg reference, ~10 at 1mg. Reconstituted solution keeps ~30 days refrigerated (see storage), so match recon volume/dose to how fast you will use it.
- [ANECDOTAL] Some community "loading" or daily protocols circulate (peptidedosages lists a once-daily 500→1500mcg titration over 8–16 weeks). Flag clearly: daily dosing is NOT the approved pattern and drives much higher hyperpigmentation risk (see Side effects).

## 4. Supplies (supply-grid: 4 cards + footnote)
- **Vial(s):** 1 × PT-141 10mg (covers ~5–20 doses depending on dose).
- **Bacteriostatic water:** 1 × 30mL BAC (2mL per vial recon; plenty of headroom).
- **Syringes:** U-100 insulin syringes (0.5mL/29–31G) — need to draw up to ~35–40 units for reference/upper doses.
- **Alcohol swabs:** for vial stopper + injection site.
- Footnote: rotate subQ sites; store reconstituted vial refrigerated.
- General supplies `<ul>`: sharps bin, gauze, optional second (smaller) syringe for micro-doses.

## 5. Dosing & Reconstitution
### Reconstitution
- Recommended: **10mg vial + 2mL BAC water = 5mg/mL (5000mcg/mL)**.
- Add BAC slowly down the vial wall; swirl gently, do NOT shake; let fully dissolve.
- Refrigerate (2–8°C) after mixing; use within ~30 days; avoid freeze–thaw. Store lyophilised vial cold/dark until use.
- Recon options (choose by preferred syringe volume):

| BAC water | Concentration | 1.75mg dose = | in units |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1mL | 10mg/mL | 0.175mL | 17–18 u |
| 2mL (recommended) | 5mg/mL | 0.35mL | 35 u |
| 3mL | 3.33mg/mL | 0.525mL | ~53 u |

### Injectable dosing table (at recommended 5mg/mL)

| Level | Dose | Volume | U-100 units | Doses per 10mg vial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 0.5mg | 0.10mL | 10 u | ~20 |
| Moderate | 1.0mg | 0.20mL | 20 u | ~10 |
| Reference (Vyleesi) | 1.75mg | 0.35mL | 35 u | ~5–6 |
| Upper | 2.0mg | 0.40mL | 40 u | ~5 |

### Formula
- **Volume (mL) = dose (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/mL)**
- **U-100 units = Volume (mL) × 100**
- **Doses per vial = vial mg ÷ dose mg**

### Worked example
- 10mg vial + 2mL BAC → 5mg/mL.
- Want 1.75mg: 1.75 ÷ 5 = **0.35mL** → ×100 = **35 units**.
- Doses per vial: 10 ÷ 1.75 ≈ **5.7 → ~5 usable doses**.

### Timing & kinetics (cited — see Research)
- Inject **≥45 min before** anticipated activity (label instruction).
- Tmax ~1h; half-life ~2.7h → on-demand window of a few hours; subQ bioavailability ~100%.
- Do not re-dose within 24h if effect feels weak — wait.

## 6. Alternative Protocols (h3 per variant)
- **Nasal spray** — cross-link to PT-141 nasal spray page. [MOSTLY ANECDOTAL for modern research use] Historical intranasal trials (Diamond et al.) showed activity but **substantially lower and more variable bioavailability** than subQ; intranasal development was abandoned partly over BP variability. Community nasal doses run higher per-use to compensate. Mark as less predictable.
- **Dose-finding / low-and-slow** — [ANECDOTAL] first dose 0.5mg to test nausea + BP, titrate toward 1.75–2mg over subsequent uses. Take with light food / anti-nausea plan if prone to nausea.
- **Male use (off-label)** — [RESEARCH-adjacent] early intranasal male-ED trials (7mg / 20mg IN) and PT-141 + sildenafil co-dosing showed enhanced erectile response; note Vyleesi approval is women-only, male use is off-label/community.
- **Melanotan II** — related MC agonist (tanning + libido); different risk profile, cross-link only, do not conflate.

## 7. What Is PT-141
- Synthetic cyclic heptapeptide; analogue of α-MSH; metabolite of Melanotan II.
- **Melanocortin receptor agonist** — non-selective, potency order MC1R > MC4R > MC3R > MC5R > MC2R; MC3R/MC4R are CNS receptors driving sexual response.
- Acts **centrally** (hypothalamic/limbic) on desire/arousal pathways — mechanistically distinct from PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra) which act on peripheral vasculature.
- Developed by Palatin; approved as **Vyleesi (2019)** for acquired, generalised HSDD in premenopausal women.
- Data maturity: strong human RCT data in premenopausal women (RECONNECT); male/other uses are earlier-stage or off-label.

## 8. Supplementary Notes
- **Combinations** [ANECDOTAL]: sometimes stacked with Melanotan II or used alongside PDE5 inhibitors; note additive BP/nausea concerns, not studied for safety in stacks.
- **Nausea management** [ANECDOTAL]: lower first dose, hydration, timing with food.
- **Side effects (route-split):**
  - subQ (from RECONNECT Phase 3): **nausea ~40%** (vs 1% placebo), **flushing ~20%**, injection-site reactions ~13%, **headache ~11%**.
  - **Transient BP rise:** ~6mmHg systolic / 3mmHg diastolic, peaks 2–4h, resolves by ~12h.
  - **Focal hyperpigmentation (skin darkening):** ~1% at ≤8 doses/month, but **~38% with daily dosing** — a key reason to keep on-demand, not daily. May not fully resolve.
  - Nasal: BP effects were more variable — historical concern.
- **Contraindications:** uncontrolled hypertension; known cardiovascular disease.

## 9. Research (cited themes — split from anecdote)
- **HSDD efficacy (women):** RECONNECT Phase 3 (Studies 301/302), n=1,247, 1.75mg subQ on-demand 24 wks → statistically significant rise in FSFI-desire and drop in FSDS-DAO distress vs placebo (P<0.001). Kingsberg et al., 2019.
- **Mechanism / pharmacology:** Molinoff et al., 2003 — PT-141 melanocortin agonism, dose-dependent erectile activity in men + preclinical.
- **Female arousal (intranasal):** Diamond et al., 2006 — single intranasal dose increased subjective arousal in premenopausal women with arousal disorder.
- **Regulatory anchor:** FDA Vyleesi label — 1.75mg subQ dosing, kinetics, safety, BP and hyperpigmentation warnings.
- Close with callout: "Research vs anecdote — approved data is for 1.75mg on-demand in women; other doses/routes/populations are extrapolation or community practice."

## 10. Beyond libido (melanocortin effects — Research/Anecdotal split)
- **Appetite / body weight:**
  - Research: MC4R agonism modulates energy balance; a bremelanotide study in obese women examined weight effects (referenced by peptidedosages). Melanocortin pathway is central to appetite regulation.
  - Anecdotal: some users report mild appetite suppression; not a weight-loss tool, not validated.
- **Skin pigmentation:**
  - Research: MC1R activation drives melanogenesis → the observed focal hyperpigmentation (label).
  - Anecdotal: mild tanning/freckle-darkening reported, dose/frequency-dependent.
- **Mood / motivation:**
  - Research: central melanocortin tone linked to arousal/motivation circuits (preclinical).
  - Anecdotal: some report transient mood/energy lift; unproven.
