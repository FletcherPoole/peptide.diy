## 6. Dosing & Reconstitution

### Reconstitution
- **Recommended:** 5mg vial + **2mL BAC water → 2500 mcg/mL.** Clean unit math: **1 U-100 unit (0.01mL) = 25mcg.**
- Mixing steps (numbered `<ol>`):
  1. Sanitise both vial septa and your hands.
  2. Draw 2mL BAC water into a syringe.
  3. Inject **slowly down the inside wall** of the peptide vial — never blast directly onto the powder cake (foaming/denaturation).
  4. Do **not** shake. Swirl gently / let dissolve passively until clear.
  5. Label with date; refrigerate.
- **Stability:** store reconstituted at 2-8°C (fridge); use within ~3-4 weeks. Lyophilised powder stored cool/dark before mixing. Link → Storage Guide.

### Formula
- **Volume (mL) = dose (mcg) ÷ concentration (mcg/mL)**
- **U-100 units = Volume (mL) × 100**
- **Worked example (250mcg at 2500 mcg/mL):** 250 ÷ 2500 = 0.10mL → 0.10 × 100 = **10 units.**

## 7. Alternative Protocols (h3 per variant; mark anecdotal)

### Local vs systemic (subQ)
- **Systemic:** rotate sites (abdomen, thigh, upper arm) — general/gut/whole-body effects. Site of injection thought not to matter much for systemic action (anecdotal).
- **Local:** inject subQ as close as practical to the injury (e.g. near a tendon/joint). Popular in community protocols; **not** validated in humans — animal work sometimes used intraperitoneal or intra-articular routes. Never inject into a joint space or tendon itself without clinical guidance.

### Oral / capsule
- BPC-157 is unusually stable in gastric acid — oral is plausibly active, especially for **gut** issues. Rodent gut studies used per-oral dosing (in drinking water).
- Community oral dose: ~250-500mcg 1-3x/day (capsule or reconstituted liquid swallowed). Systemic absorption is lower/uncertain vs injection — best evidence is for local GI effect. Label **anecdotal** for extra-intestinal claims.

### Nasal
- Nasal sprays marketed; no established dosing. Anecdotal, often mirrors oral/inject amounts. Flag as unvalidated.

### BPC-157 + TB-500 stack
- Common connective-tissue "repair stack". Often 1:1 to 1:5 ratio; conservative start ~1mcg/kg/day combined. Cross-link → /stacks.html for full stack protocol and combined syringe maths.

## 8. What Is BPC-157
- **BPC = Body Protection Compound.** A synthetic, stable **pentadecapeptide** (15 amino acids) — a partial sequence derived from a protein found in human gastric juice.
- Not a natural peptide as-is; it's an engineered stable fragment.
- **Research focus:** cytoprotection and tissue repair — gut lining, tendon, ligament, muscle, bone, nerve, blood vessels.
- **Proposed mechanisms:** activation of the **nitric oxide (NO) system**; promotion of **angiogenesis** via **VEGFR2 → Akt → eNOS** signalling; modulation of growth factors and the FAK-paxillin pathway; interaction with the brain-gut axis and dopaminergic/serotonergic systems.
- **Data maturity:** overwhelmingly **preclinical (rodent, in-vitro).** A large share of the literature comes from a single research group (Sikirić, University of Zagreb); independent replication is limited. Only early-phase human safety work exists (see Research).

## 9. Supplementary Notes
- **Combinations (anecdotal):** TB-500 (thymosin β4) for connective tissue; GHK-Cu for skin/wound; sometimes paired with growth-hormone secretagogues — all community practice, not evidenced.
- **Rodent-to-human dose note:** animal studies commonly use ~10mcg/kg; naive body-surface-area conversion lands near ~150mcg for a large adult — human doses in community use (250-500mcg) are extrapolations, not clinically established.
- **Toxicity ceiling:** animal studies report very high tolerability / minimal toxicity even at elevated doses — but this does **not** transfer as a human safety guarantee.
- **Side effects (route-split):**
  - *Injection:* transient local redness, swelling, soreness at site.
  - *Oral:* generally well-tolerated in reports; mild GI complaints possible.
  - *Nasal:* irritation, sneezing, coughing.
  - *General:* occasional reports of headache, lightheadedness, fatigue, nausea (anecdotal). Long-term human safety unknown; theoretical concern re: angiogenesis and tumours — unproven but cautioned.

## 10. Research (cited, linked; end with Research vs anecdote callout)

### Tendon / ligament healing (preclinical)
- Rat transected Achilles + tendon-to-bone: BPC-157 promoted healing and opposed corticosteroid impairment — Krivic et al., 2006. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16583442/
- Rat ligament (MCL) healing, PL 14736: improved healing — Cerovecki et al., 2010. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20225319/
- Tendon healing mechanism (outgrowth, cell survival/migration, in vitro) — Chang et al., 2011. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21030672/
- Myotendinous junction repair in rats (10µg/kg IP or per-oral) — 2021. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8615275/

### Angiogenesis / nitric oxide mechanism (preclinical)
- NO-system modulation in wound healing — Klicek et al. https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jphs/108/1/108_FP0072161/_article
- Vasomotor tone via Src-Caveolin-1-eNOS pathway — 2020. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-74022-y

### Reviews (preclinical synthesis)
- Multifunctionality & possible medical applications — Józwiak et al., Pharmaceuticals, 2025. https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8247/18/2/185
- Tissue repair / cytoprotection overview — Sikirić et al. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5333585/

### Human data (very limited)
- Phase I oral safety trial (registered) — NCT02637284. https://clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT02637284
- No published, adequately powered human efficacy RCTs. State plainly.

- **Callout — Research vs anecdote:** dosing bands, local-injection technique, and most extra-intestinal benefit are extrapolated from animal data and user reports. The peptide is unapproved; treat all protocols as experimental.

## 11. Beyond healing (Research / Anecdotal split per application)

### Gut (IBD, ulcers, leaky gut, GI protection)
- **Research:** strongest preclinical signal — heals gastric/intestinal lesions, fistulas, protects mucosa; oral-active in GI tract (rodent). Cite Sikirić review / MDPI 2025.
- **Anecdotal:** users report relief from reflux, IBS, NSAID gut damage; oral dosing favoured.

### Tendon / ligament / joint
- **Research:** multiple rat models show accelerated tendon/ligament/muscle-to-bone healing (above).
- **Anecdotal:** most common real-world use — elbow/knee/shoulder tendinopathy; local subQ near the injury.

### Brain-gut axis / neuro
- **Research:** rodent data on neuroprotection, counteracting encephalopathy, dopaminergic/serotonergic modulation, TBI/nerve models. Preclinical only.
- **Anecdotal:** reports of mood/anxiety and post-concussion improvements — unproven.

### Vascular / cardio
- **Research:** angiogenesis and collateral-vessel formation in ischaemia models; NO-mediated blood-pressure/vasomotor effects (rat).
- **Anecdotal:** minimal user-facing claims; keep speculative.

### Other (skin, bone, systemic recovery)
- **Research:** wound/skin and bone-healing rodent studies; broad cytoprotection theme.
- **Anecdotal:** faster training recovery, wound healing — community reports.
