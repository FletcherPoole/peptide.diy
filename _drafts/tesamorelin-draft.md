## protocol-callout (Research purposes only)

- Tesamorelin is a **prescription drug** (Egrifta / Egrifta SV), FDA-approved only for reducing excess abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy.
- Research/DIY, body-composition, anti-ageing and cognitive uses are **off-label / not approved**. Sold as research material, not for human use.
- Systemic GHRH analog — raises growth hormone and IGF-1; carries glucose/insulin and theoretical growth/malignancy considerations. Emphasise titration and medical supervision. **Not medical advice.**

## 1. At a Glance (protocol-tldr)

- **Vial:** 10mg lyophilised tesamorelin (multi-dose research vial). *Note: pharmaceutical Egrifta SV is supplied as small single-use 2mg vials — the 10mg research vial is a convenience format, not the licensed presentation.*
- **Mix:** 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water → **3.33 mg/mL**
- **Dose:** 2 mg once daily subcutaneously (approved/anchor dose); titrate from 1 mg in week 1
- **Per dose at 2 mg:** 0.60 mL = **60 units** on a U-100 syringe
- **Cycle:** 12–26 weeks daily; trial data extend to 52 weeks with monitoring
- **Timing:** evening / ~30 min before bed (aligns with nocturnal GH pulse; cognition trial dosed pre-bedtime)
- **Route:** subcutaneous, abdomen (rotate sites, ≥5 cm from navel)
- **Days per vial:** ~5 days at 2 mg; ~10 days at 1 mg
- **Consider:** fasting glucose / HbA1c and IGF-1 monitoring; caution with diabetes, active malignancy, cancer history

## 4. Dosing & Reconstitution

### Reconstitution
- Recommended: **3.0 mL BAC water into the 10mg vial → 3.33 mg/mL** (clean round volume, comfortable syringe draw)
- Mixing steps (`<ol>`):
  1. Wipe both vial stoppers with an alcohol swab; let dry.
  2. Draw 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water.
  3. Insert needle at an angle; let water run **slowly down the vial wall** (do not jet onto the powder — avoids foaming/denaturing).
  4. Do **not** shake. Gently swirl/roll until fully dissolved and clear.
  5. Label with date; store refrigerated.
- Stability: reconstituted with BAC water, store **2–8 °C, use within ~7 days**; avoid freeze–thaw. Lyophilised powder: keep cold/frozen per supplier; some newer formulations tolerate room temp short-term. Link Storage Guide.

### Injectable dosing table (`Level | Daily dose | Volume | U-100 units | Days per vial`)

| Level | Daily dose | Volume | U-100 units | Days per vial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titration | 1 mg | 0.30 mL | 30 | ~10 |
| Standard (approved) | 2 mg | 0.60 mL | 60 | ~5 |

### Formula
- **Volume (mL) = dose (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/mL)**
- **U-100 units = Volume (mL) × 100**
- At 3.33 mg/mL: **1 unit (0.01 mL) = 33.3 mcg**

### Worked example (10mg vial, 3.0 mL BAC, 2 mg dose)
- Concentration = 10 mg ÷ 3.0 mL = **3.33 mg/mL**
- Volume = 2 mg ÷ 3.33 mg/mL = **0.60 mL**
- Units = 0.60 × 100 = **60 units**
- Days per vial = 10 mg ÷ 2 mg = **5 days**

## 6. What Is Tesamorelin (overview)

- Synthetic analog of human **GHRH (1-44)** with an added trans-3-hexenoyl group at the N-terminus for stability/half-life; also called **TH9507**.
- **Mechanism:** binds pituitary GHRH receptors → stimulates endogenous GH secretion → raises **IGF-1** and IGFBP-3. Physiological, feedback-controlled GH release.
- **Discovery/approval:** developed by Theratechnologies; FDA-approved 2010 as **Egrifta** (later **Egrifta SV**) for HIV-associated lipodystrophy (excess visceral abdominal fat).
- **Research focus:** selective **visceral fat (VAT)** reduction; secondary interest in liver fat/NAFLD, lipids, and cognition.
- **Data maturity:** unusually strong for a "research peptide" — multiple randomised placebo-controlled **human** phase-3 trials (HIV population) plus a cognition RCT. General/healthy-population and long-term body-composition use remains off-label/under-studied.

## 7. Supplementary Notes

- **Combinations (anecdotal):** GHRH + GHRP synergy is a common community rationale; unproven in controlled trials. Keep total GH-axis stimulation conservative.
- **Balance / interactions:** additive with anything raising IGF-1; watch glucose-raising interactions (steroids, GH). May reduce insulin sensitivity — relevant for diabetics/pre-diabetics.
- **Contraindications (from label):** active malignancy, disruption of hypothalamic-pituitary axis, hypersensitivity, pregnancy.
- **Side effects (route-split / systemic):**
  - **Injection-site:** erythema, pruritus, pain, bruising, rash (most common category).
  - **Musculoskeletal:** **arthralgia**, myalgia, extremity pain; **fluid retention** → peripheral oedema, and **carpal tunnel** symptoms (fluid-related, often reversible).
  - **Metabolic:** small rises in blood glucose / **HbA1c**; label notes increased risk of developing diabetes (HbA1c ≥6.5%: 5% vs 1% placebo).
  - **Endocrine:** IGF-1 elevation — monitor; consider discontinuation if persistently >3 SDS above normal.
- Toxicity/ceiling: dosing above 2 mg/day is not supported by approval data and raises IGF-1/glucose risk without demonstrated benefit.

## 8. Research (cited h3 themes — CITED, URL in bullet)

### Visceral fat (strongest evidence)
- **Falutz et al., NEJM 2007** — phase-3 RCT, 412 HIV patients, 2 mg SC daily × 26 wk: **VAT −15.2%** vs **+5.0%** placebo; effect confined to visceral compartment (subcutaneous/limb fat largely unchanged); triglycerides improved. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18057339/
- **Falutz et al. (pooled analysis), J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2010** — two phase-3 trials + safety extension; confirmed VAT reduction and durability; VAT rebounds toward baseline within ~3 months of stopping. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20554713/

### Metabolic / lipids / liver
- **Stanley et al., 2012** — reduction in visceral adiposity associated with improved metabolic profile in HIV patients on tesamorelin. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22495074/
- **VAT reduction ↔ improved liver enzymes, 2017** — visceral fat loss with tesamorelin associated with improved liver enzymes/hepatic fat in HIV. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28832410/

### IGF-1 / GH axis
- Mechanism supported across trials: tesamorelin raises GH → **IGF-1 and IGFBP-3** (Egrifta SV label; pooled analysis above). IGF-1 restored toward young-adult range in the cognition trial.

### Cognition (exploratory)
- **Baker et al., Arch Neurol 2012** — RCT, 152 older adults (incl. 66 MCI), 20 wk GHRH (tesamorelin) SC pre-bedtime: improved **executive function**, trend to verbal memory; attenuated expected decline in MCI. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22869065/

### Safety references
- **LiverTox (NIH NIDDK)** — tesamorelin safety/hepatotox profile. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548730/
- **Egrifta SV Prescribing Information (FDA/DailyMed)** — dose, warnings, adverse reactions. https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=3d783378-b02d-4f19-99dd-0fc91a042224

- End with callout **"Research vs anecdote"**: human RCT evidence is HIV-population-specific; general/healthy-adult body-composition and long-term use are extrapolation, not proven.

## 9. Beyond visceral fat (Research/Anecdotal split per application)

### Liver fat / NAFLD
- **Research:** In HIV patients, tesamorelin reduced hepatic fat and improved liver enzymes without worsening glucose in dedicated trials (see Research citations). Signal is genuine but population-specific.
- **Anecdotal:** Off-label interest for general NAFLD/"metabolic" use — no approval, no non-HIV RCT confirmation.

### Cognition / healthy ageing
- **Research:** One 20-week RCT (Baker 2012) showed executive-function gains in older adults (healthy + MCI). Small, single trial, not replicated at scale.
- **Anecdotal:** Community use for "focus"/nootropic effect — labelled anecdotal; not a validated indication.

### Body composition / anti-ageing
- **Research:** GH/IGF-1 elevation is documented; selective VAT loss is proven in HIV. No RCT for physique/anti-ageing in healthy adults.
- **Anecdotal:** Reported improved midsection definition, sleep, skin — clearly anecdotal, confounded by diet/training; IGF-1 elevation is a real safety trade-off.

## 11. FAQ / House verdict

> *[HOUSE VIEW] — the site owner's candid, opinionated take. It is deliberately kept separate from the cited clinical evidence in §8–§10. Educational, not medical advice.*

- **Is tesamorelin a "crazy" fat-loss or muscle-building peptide?**
  - No. Anyone who tells you it will do crazy things for fat loss or muscle growth is mostly lying. In our view it is inferior to **Ipamorelin** and **Hexarelin** in every measurable way. **Sermorelin** shares the problem — overhyped and under-delivers.

- **So what should I use instead?**
  - Use **Ipamorelin** or **Hexarelin** (GH secretagogues / ghrelin-mimetics — cleaner, in our experience, for the same GH-axis goal). For best results, just use **HGH** directly. Tesamorelin only stimulates *your own* GH; HGH is the thing itself.

- **Does tesamorelin have any unique benefit?**
  - The one thing users consistently report is **disproportionate abdominal ("visceral") fat loss** — and this is the part the clinical data actually support (see §8, Falutz trials). But be clear-eyed: **all** HGH-axis peptides do this to some degree, and several do it with **fewer side effects** than tesamorelin — **HGH**, **HGH FRAG 176-191**, and **AOD-9604** among them. So the "unique" benefit isn't really unique; it's just the best-documented.

- **Then why does tesamorelin get any credit here at all?**
  - Because honesty cuts both ways. Tesamorelin is the **only GHRH analog with FDA approval and randomised placebo-controlled human trial data** for visceral-fat reduction (§8–§9). If your specific goal is reducing visceral abdominal fat — and especially HIV-associated lipodystrophy, its licensed use — it is the *most evidenced* choice on this page. We're skeptical of the marketing, not of the trials.

- **Bottom line?**
  - Overhyped for physique and "anti-ageing"; genuinely evidenced for visceral fat in a narrow clinical setting. If you're chasing general body composition, we'd reach for **Ipamorelin / Hexarelin / HGH** first. If you specifically want the option with the strongest RCT visceral-fat paper trail, that's tesamorelin.
