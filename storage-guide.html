Peptide Storage Guide

Before and after reconstitution — complete storage requirements for research peptides.

Before Reconstitution (Lyophilized Powder)

Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides are significantly more stable than reconstituted solutions. With proper storage, most peptides retain potency for months to years in this form.

Ideal Long-Term Storage

  • Refrigerate at 2–8°C (36–46°F)
  • Keep away from direct light
  • Avoid temperature fluctuations
  • Some peptides can be frozen for extended storage
  • Most remain stable for 12–24 months when refrigerated

Avoid These Conditions

  • Direct sunlight or UV exposure
  • Heat above 25°C (77°F) for extended periods
  • High humidity environments
  • Repeated temperature cycling
  • Storing near appliances that emit heat

After Reconstitution (Liquid Solution)

Once reconstituted, peptides begin degrading and must be handled carefully. The benzyl alcohol in BAC water extends shelf life significantly, but does not prevent all degradation.

Required Conditions

  • Refrigerate at 2–8°C immediately after reconstitution
  • Use within 25–30 days (with BAC water)
  • Use within 24–48 hours (with sterile water)
  • Store vial upright at all times
  • Keep away from direct light

Critical Don'ts

  • Never freeze a reconstituted peptide — ice crystals destroy peptide bonds
  • Never shake the vial — causes degradation and foaming
  • Don't leave at room temperature — even briefly degrades potency
  • Don't use after 30 days — even if it looks fine

Labeling Your Vials

Always label reconstituted vials with:

  • The peptide name
  • The date of reconstitution
  • The volume of BAC water added
  • The resulting concentration (optional but helpful)

Use small adhesive labels or tape. Permanent marker works well on glass vials. This prevents accidental use of expired solution and helps you track dosing.

Shipping & Transport

When receiving peptides by mail:

  • Inspect immediately on arrival — check for any signs of damage, unusual color, or broken vials
  • Refrigerate lyophilized peptides as soon as possible after arrival
  • Brief room-temperature exposure during shipping is generally acceptable for lyophilized powder but is best minimized
  • Do not use peptides that arrived visibly damaged, discolored, or with compromised vial integrity
