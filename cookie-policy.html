Cookie Policy

How cookies are used on peptide.diy.

Last updated: May 2026

About This Site

peptide.diy is a free research-education website. We use cookies only to operate and understand how the Site is used. We do not use cookies to sell products, process payments, peptide orders, or deliver medical services, because we are not a vendor, accept no money, and do not provide clinical care.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can remember preferences and help site owners understand traffic patterns.

Cookies We May Use

Strictly necessary: Basic cookies that allow the Site to function (e.g. security or load balancing where applicable).

Analytics: We may use tools such as Google Analytics to collect aggregated data about pages visited, session duration, and referral sources. This helps us improve research content and site structure. Analytics cookies do not give us your medical information or purchase history on this Site.

Preferences: If you subscribe to a newsletter or save site preferences in the future, a cookie may remember those settings.

Third-Party Cookies

Analytics and embedded services may set their own cookies. We do not control third-party cookies. Review the relevant provider's privacy policy for details and opt-out options.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse all cookies or only third-party cookies. Blocking analytics cookies will not prevent you from reading Site content, though we may have less insight into how to improve it.

Google Analytics opt-out (if enabled): Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

Do Not Track

We do not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals in a uniform way. You can still limit cookies through browser controls.

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy. Check the date above for the latest version. See also our Privacy Policy.
