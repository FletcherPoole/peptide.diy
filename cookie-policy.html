Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on peptide.diy.

Last updated: January 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

How We Use Cookies

Analytics cookies: We use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site — which pages are most visited, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve content.

Preference cookies: If you subscribe to our newsletter or set any site preferences, a cookie may be used to remember those settings.

Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies or only third-party cookies. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect the core functionality of this site.

Third-Party Cookies

Analytics providers may set their own cookies. We have no control over these cookies; refer to each provider's privacy policy for details.
