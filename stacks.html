Peptide Stack Protocols

Separate vials, one schedule — stacks let you tune dose and timing for each compound instead of locking into a pre-mixed ratio.

Stack vs blend: A blend is one vial with a fixed ratio — reconstitute once, dose once. A stack uses multiple vials on a coordinated plan so you can run different amounts, frequencies, injection sites, or routes (injectable vs topical) for each compound. Choose a stack when flexibility matters more than convenience.
