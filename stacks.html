Peptide Stack Protocols

Multiple compounds in one schedule for a specific purpose, allowing different deloads and dosage ramps for optimal results.

Stack vs blend

Two ways to combine peptides. Pick a stack when you need separate control over each compound.

Blend

One vial, fixed ratio. Reconstitute once and every dose delivers the same proportion of each peptide.

Stack

Multiple vials on one schedule. Set dose, timing, deloads, and ramps for each compound on its own.
