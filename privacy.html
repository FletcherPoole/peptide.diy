Privacy Policy

How we handle information on a research-education site.

Last updated: May 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to peptide.diy, an educational website that compiles research-related information about peptides. We are not a vendor and do not process orders, payments, prescriptions, or shipments for peptides or related products. No money is paid to peptide.diy for use of the Site. We collect only the data needed to operate the Site and communicate with visitors who contact us or subscribe to updates.

Information We Collect

Automatically collected technical data: When you visit the Site, we or our analytics providers may collect IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from IP. See our Cookie Policy.

Information you provide voluntarily: If you use our contact form, we receive whatever you submit (name, email, message content). If you subscribe to a newsletter, we collect your email address.

What we do not collect: We do not collect health records, prescription information, payment card data for product purchases, or shipping addresses for peptide orders, because we do not sell products.

How We Use Information

  • Operate, secure, and improve the Site
  • Respond to contact enquiries
  • Send newsletters to subscribers who opt in
  • Understand aggregate traffic patterns through analytics

We do not use your information to provide medical advice, clinical services, or product fulfilment.

Legal Bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we process data based on legitimate interests (site operation and security), consent (newsletter signup), or performance of a request you initiate (responding to contact messages).

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Analytics providers (e.g. Google Analytics) under their policies
  • Email delivery services if you subscribe to updates
  • Authorities if required by law or to protect rights and safety

Retention

Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are retained long enough to respond and handle follow-up, then deleted unless we must keep them for legal reasons. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics provider's settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website is completely secure. Do not send sensitive medical or financial information through the contact form.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Visitors

If you access the Site from outside our hosting region, your data may be processed in countries with different privacy laws.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The revised date will appear at the top. Material changes may be noted on the Site.

Contact

Privacy questions: Contact page.
