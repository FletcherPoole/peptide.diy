Privacy Policy

How we collect and use your information.

Last updated: January 2026

Information We Collect

When you use peptide.diy, we may collect the following types of information: technical information (IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited) through cookies and analytics tools; email address if you subscribe to our newsletter; and any information you voluntarily submit through our contact form.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the website, send newsletters to subscribers who have opted in, respond to contact form submissions, and analyze site traffic to understand which content is most useful.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics purposes (to understand how users navigate the site) and, if implemented, for newsletter subscription preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; this may affect certain site functionality. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share technical data with analytics providers (like Google Analytics) under their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention

Newsletter subscriber emails are retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are retained for a reasonable period to respond to your inquiry, then deleted.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us via the Contact page to exercise these rights.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the Contact page.
